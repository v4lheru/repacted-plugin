# Protocol: self-refine (skill-specific part)

Context: this skill decays when a new document format, corpus trap or claim shape bites and nobody
records it. Run this at the end of a session that used the skill.

Run the shared protocol [protocols/self-refine.md](../../../protocols/self-refine.md); its step 5 is the list below. Everything else (mission, log entry, validation, report) is shared.

## Skill-specific steps
- Review the session: which count, quote, extraction or claim was wrong or nearly wrong, and which
   rule here caught it or failed to.
- New detail goes in the reference for its family (transcripts, corpus and documents, claims,
   reports).
- Write the rule with generic referents ("a person's nickname", "a client workbook"): never a
   colleague's, client's or stakeholder's name, email or a de-anonymising detail.
- Stamp a dated market or product fact `(as of <YYYY-MM>)` so a reader knows when to re-check it.

## Skill-specific guidelines
- Anti-pattern: restoring the absolute read-whole form ("every payload, any project"); this skill narrows
  it to decision-driving documents and payloads you reason from or quote.
